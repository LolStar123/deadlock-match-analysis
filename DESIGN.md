# Deadlock match workbench

## Audience and job

A player or analyst asks how often a team leading in a stat wins. The primary action is changing the condition, with its sample size and uncertainty always visible beside the win rate.

## Design references and signature

The internal references are the archive's real checkpoint/outcome fields and the existing match-stat comparison flow. They supply the vocabulary and comparison structure. The signature is a horizontal interval plot with empirical lead ranges and an aligned win-rate/sample column. No competitor identity or copy is imported.

Desktop uses a 260 px condition column and an expanding analysis area inside a 1120 px frame. Mobile moves the condition above the answer, gives the primary rate its own column beside sample/interval, and keeps 0%, 50% and 100% axis labels aligned with fixed-size dots. Chart coordinates are SVG percentages, without a stretched viewBox.

## Density and truthful states

Ranges with fewer than ten observations move into an expandable **small cohorts** section. Bins, win rates, sample sizes and Wilson intervals remain unchanged. The default ten-minute condition has four ordinary ranges and eight small ranges containing 23 observations; small samples do not occupy most of the first screen.

Duration ranges are half-open, preventing matches at exactly 25 or 40 minutes appearing in two cohorts. Missing/tied exclusions are counted separately. Empty cohorts have no invented percentage and disable export. A failed data load leaves controls disabled and supplies a reload action. Match inspection resets when its cohort changes.

Spacing uses 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32 px steps, with one bordered workbench and quiet dividers. The fixed historical census is labelled beside the title. Association/forecast boundaries sit with the condition, rather than being repeated in several long paragraphs.

## Shared implementation rules

Fonts come from the operating system; no font files or external requests are bundled. Layout starts from the working product, with no marketing cards, decorative gradients, scroll reveals or invented statistics. Code-native artwork stays editable. Controls use at least 44 px hit areas where practical and a visible two-pixel keyboard outline. Reduced motion disables decorative animation; essential state changes still happen.

Local acceptance: 1280 ? 1000 desktop and 390 ? 844 mobile, no horizontal page overflow, main flow and export, keyboard focus, unavailable/error states, and no uncaught page errors. README previews and ignored `output/qa/` screenshots are generated by the actual browser audits. Screenshots were visually inspected; the review is a solo craft check, not user testing.
