what the comparison means

We identify the team leading the chosen metric, then count how often that team won. Ties and missing checkpoints are excluded. The interval is a 95% Wilson interval; it expresses sampling uncertainty, not causal certainty.

Changing the question after seeing the result can exaggerate findings. Match conditions, heroes and player skill still matter.
